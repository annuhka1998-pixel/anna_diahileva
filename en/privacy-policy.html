Version of 13 November 2026

1. General provisions

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out how the personal data of users of the AndiStudio website (the “Website”) and of clients who contact us for website development services is processed and protected.

The Policy has been drawn up in accordance with the Constitution of Ukraine, the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, the Law of Ukraine “On Electronic Commerce”, the Law of Ukraine “On Information Protection in Information and Communication Systems” and other legal acts of Ukraine.

By using the Website, submitting a request form or contacting us via messengers or email, you confirm that you have read this Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with the terms of the Policy, please do not send us your personal data.

2. Definitions

Personal data: information or a set of information about an individual who is identified or can be specifically identified.

Processing of personal data: any action or set of actions, including collection, registration, accumulation, storage, adaptation, modification, renewal, use, dissemination, depersonalization and destruction of personal data.

Personal data controller: the person who determines the purpose of processing personal data, the composition of such data and the procedures for processing it.

Personal data processor: a person whom the controller has authorized to process personal data on its behalf.

Personal data subject: the individual whose personal data is processed, that is, a user of the Website or a client.

Cookies: small text files stored on your device when you visit the Website.

3. Who processes your data

Personal data controller: Hanna Diahileva, registered sole proprietor (FOP), taxpayer number 3615107701 (the “Controller”), operating under the name AndiStudio.

You can contact the Controller with any questions about the processing of personal data by email at annuhka1998@gmail.com or on Telegram at @anna_diahileva.

4. What data we receive

We process only the data you provide yourself via the request form, messengers or email:

  • name
  • phone number or Telegram username
  • email address
  • your business name, links to your website or social media
  • information about your project that you provide in your request or correspondence

If we enter into a contract, we may additionally process the data needed to prepare it and receive payment: full name, details of the sole proprietor or company, and payment details for issuing invoices.

We do not request or collect passport data, bank card data or other sensitive data through the Website.

5. Data collected automatically

When you visit the Website, the hosting provider and analytics services may automatically receive technical data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, interface language, pages you viewed, referral source, and date and time of the visit.

This data is used in depersonalized form for statistics, security and improving the Website, and is not used to identify you.

6. Cookies

The Website may use cookies:

  • necessary: ensure the Website works correctly
  • analytical: help us understand how visitors use the Website and which pages they view most often

You can delete cookies or block them in your browser settings at any time. In that case, some features of the Website may not work correctly.

7. Why we use data

  • to contact you and respond to your request
  • to hold a consultation, prepare a commercial proposal, and estimate the price and timeline
  • to conclude and perform a contract and agree on work stages
  • to send invoices, acts and other project documents
  • to provide support after the website launch
  • to analyze and improve the Website

We do not use your data for advertising mailings without your separate consent and do not sell it to third parties.

8. Grounds for processing

In accordance with Article 11 of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, we process data on the following grounds:

  • your consent, which you give by submitting the request form or contacting us
  • the conclusion and performance of a contract to which you are a party
  • the performance of obligations established by law, in particular accounting and tax records
  • the legitimate interest of the Controller in ensuring the security and stable operation of the Website

9. How processing takes place

Personal data is processed using electronic means: email services, messengers, cloud document storage and request management systems.

We process data to the minimum extent necessary to achieve the stated purposes and do not combine it with data from other sources to create your profile.

10. Who else may have access to the data

Data may be stored and processed in the services we use for our work: Website hosting, email, messengers (Telegram, Viber, WhatsApp), cloud document storage, analytics services, and banking and payment services. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

Members of the AndiStudio team working on your project have access to the data only to the extent necessary to perform the work and are obliged to keep it confidential.

We may transfer data to state authorities only in the cases and in the manner expressly provided for by the laws of Ukraine.

11. Cross-border data transfer

Some of the services we use (email services, messengers, cloud storage, analytics) may store data on servers outside Ukraine. By sending us your data, you agree to such a transfer. We choose services that provide an adequate level of personal data protection.

12. How long data is stored

Data from requests that did not lead to cooperation is stored for no longer than 1 year from the last contact.

Data of clients with whom a contract has been concluded is stored for the term of the contract and afterwards for as long as required by law, in particular for accounting and tax purposes.

After the storage period expires, the data is deleted or depersonalized.

13. How we protect data

We take organizational and technical measures to protect personal data against accidental loss, destruction, unlawful processing and unauthorized access:

  • only those who need it for work have access to the data
  • the Website uses a secure connection (SSL)
  • service accounts are protected with passwords and two-factor authentication where possible
  • we do not publish your data publicly

14. Your rights

In accordance with Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • submit a reasoned request to change or delete your data if it is processed unlawfully or is inaccurate
  • protect your data against unlawful processing, accidental loss or destruction
  • withdraw your consent to the processing of personal data
  • file complaints with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or with a court
